Why Homeowners Choose Clacy Concrete

Built from the Ground Up: Sub-Base Prep, Rebar & Precision Finishing

Any concrete contractor can pour wet concrete onto dirt, smooth it over, and leave before the real problems start. At Clacy Concrete, we know that long-lasting concrete in Melbourne, VIC is won or lost beneath the surface long before the ready-mix truck arrives.

Freeze-thaw cycles, expansive clay soils, heavy vehicles, and seasonal rainfall put enormous stress on exterior flatwork. That is why our crews excavate to proper depth, install and mechanically compact a crushed-stone sub-base, tie #3 or #4 steel rebar grids on chairs, and place 4,000+ PSI air-entrained concrete with strategically saw-cut control joints.

From your initial site walk-through to the final sealer coat and job-site cleanup, you work directly with an experienced project lead who communicates clearly, respects your landscaping, and delivers on schedule.

How We Work

Our Proven 4-Step Concrete Installation Process

We keep your concrete project straightforward, transparent, and stress-free from the first phone call to the final walkthrough.

1

On-Site Measurement & Quote

We visit your property in Melbourne or Gippsland & South East Melbourne, evaluate grading and drainage, discuss finish options, and deliver a clear, itemized written estimate with zero hidden fees.

2

Excavation & Base Compaction

Our crew removes old concrete or sod, grades for positive water runoff away from your home, and compacts a crushed-gravel base so your new slab never settles unevenly.

3

Forming, Rebar & Precision Pour

We set laser-leveled formwork, install steel rebar or wire mesh reinforcement, and pour high-strength ready-mix concrete tailored to your project's load requirements.

4

Finishing, Control Joints & Sealing

After hand-troweling, brooming, or stamping, we cut clean control joints to manage natural shrinkage, apply protective curing sealers, and leave your yard spotless.
